# RTChess Gameplay Demonstration

## 🎮 How to Experience All Features

### **Step 1: Launch the Game**
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. You'll see the main menu with "RTChess: The General's Gambit"
3. Click **"Start New Game"**

### **Step 2: Planning Phase**
1. **See the Board**: 8×8 checkerboard with all 32 pieces
   - White pieces (light pink) at bottom
   - Black pieces (dark gray) at top
   - Each piece shows its type (K=King, Q=Queen, R=Rook, etc.)

2. **Select a Piece**: Click any white piece
   - Green highlight appears around selected piece
   - Mission Control panel shows current settings

3. **Give Movement Orders**: 
   - Click an empty square to set movement target
   - Red line shows the planned path
   - Try selecting a pawn and clicking 2 squares forward

4. **Set Rules of Engagement**:
   - Select a piece near the center
   - Change ROE to "Opportunistic" 
   - Change Pace to "Forced March"
   - Click "Set Order"

5. **Try Command Cards**:
   - Click "Forward Scouts" card
   - Notice the crosshair cursor
   - Click anywhere on the board to activate

### **Step 3: Start the Battle**
1. Click **"START BATTLE"**
2. **Watch the Action**:
   - Pieces begin moving toward their targets
   - Green circle around your King shows command aura
   - Fog of war covers areas outside your vision

### **Step 4: Real-Time Command**
1. **Move Your King**: Click empty squares to reposition
   - Command aura moves with the King
   - Notice how it affects order delivery

2. **Give New Orders**:
   - Click a piece to select it
   - Change its orders in Mission Control
   - If within aura: instant order
   - If outside aura: yellow messenger line appears

3. **Use Command Cards**:
   - Click "Rallying Cry" 
   - Click near your pieces to boost morale
   - Watch pieces get stronger in combat

### **Step 5: Watch Combat**
1. **Engage the Enemy**:
   - Move pieces toward black pieces
   - Watch automatic combat when they meet
   - See pieces get eliminated or routed

2. **Observe AI Behavior**:
   - Pieces with "Engage on Sight" attack automatically
   - "Hold Fire" pieces only defend
   - "Opportunistic" pieces only attack when stronger

### **Step 6: Advanced Features**
1. **Fog of War**:
   - Notice you can only see near your pieces
   - King has extended forward vision
   - Use "Forward Scouts" card to reveal all

2. **Messenger Interception**:
   - Send orders to distant pieces
   - Sometimes messengers get intercepted
   - Yellow lines disappear when intercepted

3. **Mission Types**:
   - Try setting "Hold Position" for defensive pieces
   - Use "Support" to protect important pieces
   - Set up "Patrol" routes for scouts

### **Step 7: Victory**
1. **Capture the Enemy King**:
   - Focus attacks on the black King
   - Use superior numbers and positioning
   - Game ends when King is eliminated

2. **Game Over Screen**:
   - Winner announcement appears
   - Click "Play Again" to restart

## 🎯 Key Features to Notice

### **Visual Indicators**:
- 🟢 Green highlight = Selected piece
- 🔴 Red line = Movement target
- 🟢 Green circle = King's command aura
- 🟡 Yellow line = Active messenger
- 🌫️ Dark overlay = Fog of war
- ⚔️ Piece collision = Combat

### **Strategic Elements**:
- **Command Aura**: Stay close to King for instant orders
- **Fog of War**: Position pieces for maximum vision
- **Card Timing**: Save powerful cards for key moments
- **ROE Settings**: Match behavior to tactical situation
- **Messenger Risk**: Balance speed vs. security

### **Combat Dynamics**:
- **Power = Strength × Stamina × Morale**
- **2:1 ratio = Elimination**
- **1.2:1 ratio = Routing**
- **<1.2:1 ratio = Mutual damage**

## 🏆 Mastery Tips

1. **Use Your King Actively**: Move to where orders are needed most
2. **Manage Fog of War**: Position scouts for maximum intelligence
3. **Time Your Cards**: Save game-changers for critical moments
4. **Protect Messengers**: Clear paths before sending orders
5. **Combined Arms**: Use different piece types together
6. **Morale Matters**: Keep troops confident for better combat
